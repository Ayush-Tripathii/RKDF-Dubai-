Who regulates us

Three layers of accountability

RKDF University Dubai answers to the Government of Dubai, to the federal Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, and to the academic regulations of its home university in India.

KHDA

Knowledge and Human Development Authority

Educational Permit No. DXB-HE-2026-0147

The Dubai Government authority responsible for the quality of higher education within the emirate.

The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) regulates private education in Dubai. Higher-education institutions operating in Dubai’s free zones, including Dubai International Academic City, must hold a KHDA permit.

KHDA reviews each institution’s academic quality, governance and student services, and confirms that programmes are recognised by the institution’s home country.

MoHESR / CAA

Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research — Commission for Academic Accreditation

Initial Institutional Licensure — application in progress

The federal authority. Licensure enables attestation of your degree for employment across the UAE and the wider Gulf.

The Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA), part of the UAE Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, licenses higher-education institutions and accredits their programmes at federal level.

Federal licensure and programme accreditation allow degrees to be attested by the Ministry, which is commonly required for federal government employment and for some professional licences in the UAE.

Your degree

Attestation, equivalency and employment

Attestation is the official confirmation, by a government authority, that a degree certificate is genuine and was issued by a recognised institution. In the UAE, the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research attests degrees for employment and further study.

Degrees awarded by institutions holding federal licensure and programme accreditation from the CAA can be attested by the Ministry. RKDF University Dubai’s application for Initial Institutional Licensure is in progress; we will publish each milestone on this page as it is reached.

An equivalency certificate confirms that a degree is equivalent to a UAE degree at the same level. It may be needed for some public-sector roles and regulated professions. The Ministry issues it on application.

Degrees are awarded under the academic regulations of RKDF University, a UGC-recognised university. Recognition of degrees delivered outside India is governed by UGC regulations on offshore campuses, and we will publish the applicable position for each programme.

Each GCC state has its own recognition process. In most cases employers and regulators look first for home-country recognition and UAE federal attestation. Our careers team can advise on specific countries and professions.

Independent checks

How to verify our status

You should never rely on a university’s own description of its status. Each of our regulators publishes the information you need to check it independently.

  • KHDA. Search for “RKDF University Dubai” in the list of permitted higher-education providers on the KHDA website, khda.gov.ae.
  • MoHESR / CAA. Contact the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research or consult its published list of licensed institutions at mohesr.gov.ae.
  • RKDF University, Bhopal. Confirm the university’s status on the UGC list of state private universities (ugc.gov.in) and on the NAAC website (naac.gov.in).

If anything you find differs from what is published on this page, please tell us at registrar@rkdfdubai.ac.ae and we will correct it.
