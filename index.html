Our Heritage

From Bhopal to Dubai

RKDF University Dubai is a campus of RKDF University, Bhopal — a private state university in Madhya Pradesh established by the Ram Krishna Dharmarth Foundation. The university is accredited by NAAC, recognised by the UGC, and teaches across seventeen faculties, from engineering, pharmacy and law to agriculture, nursing and architecture.

Its research is practical and specific. In August 2023 the university signed a memorandum of understanding with CSIR, New Delhi, at IIT Bombay. It operates a technical programme around a solar integrated carbon capture plant, leads projects sponsored by the Madhya Pradesh Council of Science and Technology on agricultural drones and precision farming, and publishes the peer-reviewed journal Shodh Sangam.

The Dubai campus inherits those standards and those habits. It will be governed by the same academic regulations, and every undergraduate may spend a semester at the home campus without additional tuition.

Why RKDF Dubai

What this campus is actually for

  1. 01

    A semester in Bhopal

    Study at the home campus with no additional tuition. Undergraduates may spend one semester in Bhopal, examined under the same regulations.

  2. 02

    Cohorts under thirty

    Every core module is taught at seminar scale. Your tutors will know your name and your work.

  3. 03

    A paid placement in every degree

    Every undergraduate degree includes a paid twelve-week placement. Our careers team finds, prepares and supports it.

  4. 04

    Tuition that stays published

    Every fee in dirhams, on one page, before you apply. Your annual tuition is fixed for the length of your programme.

  5. 05

    Faculty who still practise

    61% of our academic staff hold doctorates, and 44% maintain active industry roles. They teach what they do.

  6. 06

    Careers support for two years

    Our careers service stays with you for two years after you graduate, not two months.
